Wholesale Banking Group

Our Wholesale Banking Group continue to play its role in supporting the large corporates (domestic and international), government institutions and agencies, financial institutions and multilateral agencies to drive the overall growth and development of the real sector of the economy and nation at large. We are positioned to be your partner of first choice as we strive to live true to our name in service to our clients and the thriving Nigerian economy.

We are structured along industry and sector lines – Oil and Gas, Telecommunication, Manufacturing, Power, Utilities and Infrastructure, Services and Conglomerate, Government Institutions and Agencies, Financial Institutions and Multilateral Agencies to drive expertise. Our dedicated relationship teams are poised to provide bespoke services tailored to meet your ever-dynamic business needs as you engage the ever-changing and evolving business environment. As a leader in cash management, transaction services and trade finance, FirstBank’s relationship managers will help you optimise every opportunity, minimise the associated risks and achieve your business and organisational goals.

In addition to leveraging our financial strength, our clients benefit from our state-of -the-art, reliable and secure digital banking technology platforms assuring seamless transaction processing, while also leveraging our longstanding relationships from our long history to support business and institutional growth objectives both within and across national and international borders.
